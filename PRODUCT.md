# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hotel owners and staff evaluating and downloading hotel-management software.

## Product Purpose

Hellen Suite helps hotels and similar businesses manage operational controls and administrative tasks. This website introduces the product and helps visitors download the desktop application.

## Positioning

Hellen Suite is available as both a web application and a desktop application.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The application is under development and is not yet ready for production.
- The application and its desktop builds and releases are maintained in the separate Hellen Suite repository.
- The website uses ArixPHP and static frontend assets, without a frontend build step.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Hellen Suite.
- Hellen Suite icon assets are available in `media/` and originate from the desktop application.

## Product Principles

- Help hotel operators understand the product and find the desktop download.
- Describe the product's development status accurately.
- Keep the website aligned with the separately maintained application and its releases.
